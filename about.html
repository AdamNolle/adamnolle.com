Hey! 👋

Thanks for taking the time to check out my website and learn a little about me. So first let me introduce myself. My name is Adam Nolle. I am a graduate of Missouri University of Science and Technology (or Missouri S&T). I have a B.S. in Computer Science and Computer Engineering with a Minor in Mathematics. From January 2023 to May I was the President of my school's ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) chapter. In my time as President, I brought back our annual MegaMiner event, helped to renovate the second floor CS lounge in our school’s Computer Science building, and more!


I currently work at Accenture Federal Services (AFS) full time!

(Content) Creator 🎨

I love creating things, including digital “content” online for people to enjoy and learn from. I put most of my creative time into my YouTube channel and blog (both named Terminal Eighty) which are linked in the nav bar above! I love talking about things on my mind.


Tech Nerd 🤓

If it's technology, I am interested. I have a "Homelab", a 3D Printer, and have built 6 computers (so far, and no, not just for me). This hobby continues to grow for me which is all I can ask for.
